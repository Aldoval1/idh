Compassion and Solidarity

Compasión y Solidaridad

❮ ❯

Since high school, I have been involved in trying to make the entire community a much more inclusive place through programs like PIT Crew, which I was deeply involved in at the time. The fact that I had the opportunity to collaborate again to make my surrounding community a better place with an event like the Special Olympics fills me with pride and satisfaction. Furthermore, these types of events not only allow you to do good for the community but also enable you to develop yourself, become a much more open-minded person, and develop your social responsibility and adaptability.
